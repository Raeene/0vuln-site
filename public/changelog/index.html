Changelog

What's new on 0vuln.

New features and fixes across the Range, PacketStory and the sites. Follow along with the Atom feed.

  1. 24 Nov 2026

    PacketStory takes packets apart, byte by byte

    • Packet inspector. Click any packet to see every layer decoded: Ethernet, IPv4/IPv6, TCP, UDP, ICMP, ARP. Checksums are verified, and hovering a field highlights its exact bytes in the hex view. Use j/k to step through packets.
    • Follow conversation. Rebuilds everything two hosts said to each other, client in red, server in blue. Perfect for reading an HTTP exchange or an FTP login.
    • Solve the case. The demo capture now comes with seven investigation questions (who attacked, what was stolen, where the malware calls home) with hints that point you to the right part of the report.
    • TLS server names. HTTPS is encrypted, but the first message of every connection still names the site (SNI). PacketStory now lists those names, so a capture of normal browsing shows which servers were contacted.
    • CSV export of every packet matching your filter.
    • Works offline. After one visit, PacketStory runs without internet and can be installed as an app.
    Try it on the demo
  2. 24 Nov 2026

    The Range teaches, not just tests

    • Debriefs. Every flag explains what was misconfigured, how to fix it, what a defender would see, and the MITRE ATT&CK technique. Click a captured flag to reread it.
    • Loot notebook. Credentials and clues are written down automatically as you uncover them.
    • explain <command> describes each tool and what it looks like on the network.
    • Works offline and installs as an app.
    Open the Range
  3. 23 Nov 2026

    One design across every site

    • A shared design system for 0vuln.com, the Range, PacketStory and raeene.0vuln.com.
    • Press Ctrl K anywhere to jump between sites and pages, or run actions like loading the PacketStory demo.
    • The Range hands your session straight to PacketStory with analyze, and PacketStory now draws a network map of the busiest hosts.
  4. 22 Nov 2026

    PacketStory 2.0 and realistic Range traffic

    • PacketStory reads .pcapng as well as .pcap, with 17 detectors (up from 6), artifact tables, a timeline and a filterable packet table.
    • Range packets now have valid checksums and sequence numbers: a full playthrough opens in Wireshark with zero malformed frames.
