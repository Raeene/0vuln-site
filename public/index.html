    How it works

    Your attack becomes
    the evidence.

    Most labs stop once you have the flag. On 0vuln, every command you run generates real packets, and you investigate them next.

    01

    Attack

    Break into files01 in the Range: anonymous FTP, null sessions, a zone transfer, credentials leaked by SNMP.

    $ nmap 10.0.0.9
$ ftp 10.0.0.9
230 Login successful.
    02

    Capture

    Every command emits well-formed packets with valid checksums. Type analyze to send the whole session over.

    $ analyze
Opening PacketStory ...
Sent 3,412 packets.
    03

    Investigate

    PacketStory rebuilds your attack from the defender's side: the scan, the cleartext logins, the files you stole.

    ALERT Port scan    T1046
WARN  FTP login    T1552
ALERT Zone xfer    T1590

    Built differently

    Safe by design.
    Private by default.

    Runs in your browser

    The Range simulates its whole network in JavaScript. PacketStory parses captures in a Web Worker on your machine.

    Nothing is uploaded

    PacketStory's Content-Security-Policy is connect-src 'none', so the browser itself blocks every network request.

    Nothing real is touched

    The target is fake and lives in your tab. Practice offensive techniques without needing permission from anyone.

    Real packets

    Valid IPv4, TCP and UDP checksums, DNS and SNMP encodings. Exported sessions open cleanly in Wireshark.

    Mapped to ATT&CK

    Every detection links to its MITRE ATT&CK technique, so you learn the vocabulary defenders actually use.

    No sign-up, ever

    No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. Your Range progress is stored in your own browser.
