the loop

    
Most labs stop once you have the flag. Here, your attack becomes the evidence you investigate next.

    

      
01
attack
Hack the target in Range: recon, anonymous FTP, null sessions, zone transfers, reused passwords.

      
→

      
02
capture
Every command you run generates real packets. Type analyze to send your session over as a .pcap.

      
→

      
03
investigate
PacketStory reconstructs your attack from the defender's side: the scan, the cleartext logins, the stolen files.
