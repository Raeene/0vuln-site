    The loop

    Your attack becomes the evidence.

    Most labs end when you get the flag. Here, every command you typed was also written to a capture, and the next step is reading it.

    01

    Attack

    Break into files01 in the Range: anonymous FTP, null sessions, a zone transfer, credentials leaked by SNMP.

    $ nmap 10.0.0.9
$ ftp 10.0.0.9
230 Login successful.
    02

    Capture

    Every command emits well-formed packets with valid checksums. Type analyze to send the whole session over.

    $ analyze
Opening PacketStory ...
Sent 3,412 packets.

    Fine print

    How it's built, and what it won't do.

    where it runs
    In your tab. The Range's whole network, target included, is JavaScript. PacketStory parses captures in a Web Worker on your machine.
    uploads
    None, and the browser enforces it. PacketStory ships connect-src 'none', so even a bug couldn't send your capture anywhere.
    the packets
    Real ones. Valid IPv4/TCP/UDP checksums, TCP sequence numbers, DNS and SNMP encodings. A full playthrough opens in Wireshark with zero malformed frames.
    detections
    17 heuristics, each linked to its MITRE ATT&CK technique. They say "suspected" because they're heuristics, not magic.
    accounts
    There aren't any. No cookies, no analytics. Range progress lives in your browser's local storage and nowhere else.
    source
    Static HTML and plain JavaScript, MIT licensed, on GitHub. No framework, no build step for the tools.
